Welcome to the Catan Resource Generation Calculator!
To use this calculator, simply enter the number on each of the circular pips on the game board. (Enter a 0 for the desert.) The page will calculate the relative strengths of each potential settlement position on the board. Positions are also color-coded as follows, darker meaning stronger within each color:
Color % Chance Draws (out of 36 rolls)
Red Less than 0.75% 1-2
Yellow 0.75% to 1.5% 3-5
Blue 1.5% to 2.5% 6-8
Green 2.5% or more 9+

In short, green and blue are where you most likely want to place initial settlements, whereas red spaces are where you might want to place them if you're my buddy Chris and you "have a strategy you want to try out".

The table is pre-loaded with the sample beginner's game found in the player's rulebook.

Paste a screenshot of a colonist.io board (Ctrl/Cmd+V anywhere on this page) and the numbers are filled in and calculated for you. Any numbers it was unsure about are outlined in red, so check those.
